Delete your Rummage account and data

For the app Rummage: Fridge to Dinner, by Roland Skare

You can delete your Rummage account and all the data linked to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Rummage and tap the gear icon at the top of the screen to open Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account & data.
  3. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away, and the app starts over as a new guest.

Option 2: By email

Use this if you removed the app, lost your phone, or cannot open the app. Email roland.skare@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Rummage account" and include:

  • If you saved your account with an email: that email address. Send your request from it if you can.
  • If you used a guest account (no email): roughly when you installed and last used the app, your phone model, and a few items you remember adding. Guest accounts have no email or name, so this is how we find yours.

We may ask a question to confirm the account is yours. We confirm by email within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. If we cannot match a guest account with confidence, we will tell you.

What gets deleted

  • Your account ID, and your email address and password if you saved them
  • All photos you scanned, and any plate photos
  • Your kitchen items, grocery list, preferences, and allergy selections
  • Recipes made for you, meal plans, cooking history, ratings, and notes
  • Feedback you sent in the app and any recipe reports
  • App activity events, error reports, and your push token
  • IP address and user-agent records

What we keep

  • API request counts used for rate limits, with the link to your account removed.
  • Statistics that do not identify you.
  • Backup copies, which are deleted within 90 days.
  • If you signed up on our website to hear about the app, that name and email are stored separately. Ask in the same email and we will remove them too.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy. Questions: roland.skare@gmail.com.
