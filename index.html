Rummage — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

1. Who we are

Rummage ("Rummage," "we," "us") is a mobile app that looks at photos of your fridge and suggests dinners you can make. It is made by Roland Skare, an independent developer in New York, USA. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. It is part of our Terms of Service.

2. The short version

  • No ads and no tracking. We do not sell your information.
  • Your photos are sent to our AI providers, Google (Gemini API) and Anthropic (Claude API), to find the food in them. The app asks for your permission before it sends your first photo.
  • We do not use your photos to train or improve food recognition unless you turn on "Help improve recognition" in Settings. It is off unless you turn it on.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time in Settings.
  • Rummage is for adults 18 and older.

3. Information we collect

  • Account. A random account ID, created when you first open the app (a guest account). If you choose to save your account, your email address and a password. The password is stored only in hashed form.
  • Display name. A name you type in the app stays on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Photos. Photos you take or pick of your fridge, cupboards, groceries, or grocery receipts. They are kept in private storage tied to your account and are never shown to other users.
  • Plate photos. Some earlier test versions of the app let you add a photo of a dish you cooked. Those photos are stored at an unlisted web address. They are not shown to other users or listed anywhere, but anyone with the exact address could open them. Version 1.0 of the app does not offer this.
  • Kitchen and preferences. Food items the app finds or you add, use-by dates, household size, diet, cuisines, cooking time and skill, and allergy selections. Allergy and diet selections are used only to filter and write recipes.
  • Activity. Recipes suggested, opened, saved, planned, cooked, swapped, or skipped; ratings and cook notes; your grocery list; and searches you type.
  • Messages to us. Feedback you send in the app and emails you send us. Feedback sent in the app is private: only the developer can read it.
  • Device and usage data. Your device's push token, used to send the reminders you turn on; app events such as screens viewed, features used, and scan results; error and crash reports; and API request counts and timing. We collect this ourselves and do not use third-party advertising or analytics tools.
  • Network and device identifiers. Your IP address and device user agent from your signed-in sessions, with the first and most recent time each was seen. We use them only to detect duplicate and automated accounts, tell real users apart from our own testing, enforce rate limits, and investigate abuse. We do not use them for advertising or to find your precise location. They are deleted with your account.
  • Website sign-ups. If you leave your name and email on our website to hear about the app, we keep them until you ask us to remove them.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, or audio.

4. How we use it

  • To run the app: find food in your photos, suggest and write recipes, build plans and grocery lists, and send reminders you asked for.
  • To personalize suggestions to your kitchen and preferences.
  • To keep the app working and secure: fix errors, enforce limits, and prevent abuse.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it (see Section 5).
  • To answer your messages and requests.
  • To follow the law and protect our rights.

5. AI processing and model training

  • AI providers. We use Google (Gemini API) and Anthropic (Claude API). Either one may handle a request. They receive the photos you scan, to find your food, and your kitchen items, preferences, allergy selections, and searches, to suggest and write recipes. We do not send them your email address. They process this data to provide their service to us. Under their API terms for our paid use, they may not use this data to train their models.
  • Your permission. The app asks for your permission before it sends your first photo to these providers, and before it lets them write recipes from your kitchen and preferences. If you say not now, you still get recipes from our own collection.
  • Training on your photos is opt-in. We use your photos to train or improve food recognition only if you turn on "Help improve recognition" in Settings. It is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Other improvements. We use your edits to scan results and your activity (for example, recipes you skip or cook) to improve recognition and suggestions.
  • Troubleshooting. Whether or not that setting is on, the developer may view the photos from a scan, and what the app found in them, to fix problems and check how accurately the app recognizes food.

6. Statistics that do not identify you

We may create de-identified or aggregated statistics (for example, how often households have eggs) and use or share them for any lawful purpose. They do not identify you, and we will not try to re-identify anyone from them.

7. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • With service providers that process it for us under confidentiality obligations: Supabase, Inc. (database, file storage, and sign-in); Google LLC (Gemini API: food recognition and recipe generation); Anthropic, PBC (Claude API: food recognition and recipe generation); Expo (push notification delivery through Apple and Google); and RevenueCat, Inc. (subscription management, only if we offer a subscription and you buy one; this version has no purchases).
  • Recipe images load from Pexels and Wikimedia Commons. As with any image on the web, your device's request includes its IP address. We send them nothing else about you.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Rummage, our users, or the public.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of the app. This policy keeps applying to information collected before the transfer unless you are told otherwise.

8. How long we keep it

  • While your account exists. We keep your information, including scan photos, for as long as your account exists. Photos are not deleted automatically on a schedule today. If that changes, we will update this policy.
  • When you delete your account. Your photos, kitchen, preferences, activity, feedback, events, error reports, push token, and IP and user-agent records are deleted from our live systems right away.
  • What stays. API request counts used for rate limits are kept with the link to your account removed. De-identified statistics may be kept indefinitely. Copies in backups are deleted within 90 days.
  • Guest accounts. If you remove the app or lose your phone without saving your account, the app cannot reopen that guest account, but its data stays on our servers. Email us and we will find and delete it (see Delete your account).
  • Website sign-ups are kept until you ask us to remove them.

9. Security

We use per-account access controls, encryption in transit, and private storage for scan photos. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Delete. In the app, open Settings and tap "Delete account & data" at the bottom. If you cannot open the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • See and correct. You can view and change your kitchen, preferences, and account in the app. Email us for a copy of your data.
  • Training. Turn "Help improve recognition" on or off in Settings.
  • Notifications. Reminders are off unless you turn them on. Turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Allergy and diet selections. We use them only to filter and write recipes, and we never sell them. Remove them in Settings > Diet & restrictions, or delete your account.
  • Rights where you live. Depending on where you live (including California and the European Economic Area), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data. We honor verified requests as the law requires. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

11. Age requirement

Rummage is intended for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we delete the account and its data.

12. Where data is processed

Rummage is run from the United States, and your information is processed and stored in the United States.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and point out material changes in the app. If you keep using Rummage after a change takes effect, the updated policy applies.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: roland.skare@gmail.com. For help with the app, see Support.
