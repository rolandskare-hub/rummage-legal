Rummage — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Rummage ("the app") helps you turn what's in your kitchen into dinner plans. This policy explains what we collect and why. Short version: your data powers your own suggestions — we don't sell it and we don't run ads.

What we collect

  • Account info. An account ID, and your email if you sign up with one. Anonymous sign-in is supported.
  • Kitchen photos. Photos you take of your fridge or pantry are uploaded so our AI can identify the food in them. They're stored privately in your account and are never shared with other users.
  • Pantry and preference data. The ingredients the app tracks for you, plus preferences you set — including dietary restrictions and food allergies. Allergy settings are used only to filter recipe suggestions.
  • Cooking activity. Which suggested dinners you cook, skip, or swap, and your ratings. This tunes your future suggestions.
  • Device push token. Only if you enable notifications (dinner reminders, use-it-up alerts). You can turn these off anytime in Settings.
  • Basic usage data. API request counts and timing, used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

What we do NOT collect

No advertising identifiers, no location tracking, no contacts, no analytics SDKs harvesting your behavior, no selling or renting data to anyone — ever.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts our database and photo storage (your data is protected by per-user access rules).
  • Google (Gemini) — processes your kitchen photos to identify the food in them. Photos are sent for processing only; Google's paid API terms do not permit training on this data.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generates your recipe suggestions from your food list, and serves as the backup photo processor. Anthropic does not train on this API data.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications if you enable them.
  • Recipe photos shown in the app are served from Pexels, a stock photo service; nothing about you is sent to them beyond a standard image request.

Your controls

  • Delete your data: deleting your account removes your photos, pantry, preferences, and cooking history from our systems.
  • Notifications: opt-in, and revocable in Settings or your device settings.
  • Allergies/diet: editable or removable anytime in Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll note it in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
