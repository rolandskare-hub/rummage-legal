Delete your account and data

  1. Open Rummage and tap the gear icon at the top of the screen to open Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account & data.
  3. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, photos, kitchen, plans, and everything else linked to it. It cannot be undone. If you can no longer open the app, see Delete your account to request deletion by email.

Questions

I'm not at my fridge. How do I try it?

On the welcome screen, tap "I'm not at my fridge right now." Then open the Cook tab and tap the foods you usually have, or type items on the Food tab. Dinner ideas appear from there.

How do I get a better scan?

  • Turn the kitchen lights on and open the fridge door all the way.
  • Step back so a whole shelf fits in each photo.
  • Pull each drawer open and shoot straight down, then take one of the door shelves.
  • Just shopped? Take a photo of the receipt or the groceries on the counter.

It missed something or got an item wrong.

Open the Food tab. Use "Search, or add something we missed" to add an item, and remove anything that is wrong.

Are the recipes safe for my allergies?

Set your allergies in Settings > Diet & restrictions and we filter out dishes with them. Detection and some recipes come from AI and can be wrong, so always check ingredient labels yourself.

A recipe looks wrong or unsafe.

Open the recipe, scroll to the bottom and tap Report this recipe. You can also email us the recipe name.

Do I need an account?

No. Rummage starts a guest account on your phone. To keep your kitchen when you switch phones, open Settings, tap your account card at the top, and choose Save account to add an email and password. Then log in with that email on your new phone. A guest account cannot be reopened after you remove the app.

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are sent to our AI providers, Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude, to find your food, and are kept privately with your account. We do not use them to train food recognition unless you turn on "Help improve recognition" in Settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn dinner reminders on or off?

Settings > Dinner reminders.
